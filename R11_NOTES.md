# DevilutionX DSi R11

Tap the physical X button for its usual interaction. Hold X for 0.3 seconds during gameplay to outline ground items in bright green on the bottom display. Release X to hide the outlines. START and SELECT shortcuts still work when the modifier is pressed first.

Enable **Settings → Nintendo DSi → Music** to stream the soundtrack from your game data. Music defaults to Off. The game switches between the menu, town and dungeon tracks. Music plays in mono and can be enabled independently of sound effects. WAV tracks use PCM or Microsoft IMA ADPCM. Supply your own game archive containing the soundtrack.

For an existing R10 installation, copy the new executable over devilutionx-dsi.nds. For a fresh installation, extract the ZIP to the SD root, place DIABDAT.MPQ under sd:/devilutionx/, and launch through TWiLight Menu++ in DSi mode.

This is a hardware test build. Rendering, input, audio decoder and streaming tests ran on the host. Sound quality, timing during SD stalls, and FPS with music on still need checking on a DSi.
